02Zero friction: your subscribed agents are enough

Nothing new to buy. The wizard detects the agent CLIs you already subscribe to — Grok, Devin, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex and ten more, or OpenAI / Anthropic API keys — and orchestrates them.

wq onboard — first-run wizard

    

    
    
      
  • The wizard does three things: detects your installed CLIs, writes config from your choices, launches logins. Models, automation and submission stay off.
    • 
      
  • Honest login tracking: BRAIN and vendor CLI sign-ins are launched one by one, states recorded as-is; skip and resume anytime with wq onboard --login-only.
    • 
      
  • No baked-in paths, no scheduler: any empty directory becomes a workspace; scheduling is installed only when you explicitly enable it.
    • 
      
  • Research ≠ review: pick a single provider and the wizard tells you plainly — continuous research requires a distinct reviewer.
    •

06FAQ

  
Is this an official WorldQuant product?
No. autowq is an independent open-source project (Apache-2.0), unaffiliated with WorldQuant; BRAIN trademarks, platform and data belong to their owners.

  
Does autowq itself cost anything?
No — it's free and open source. It orchestrates your own model subscriptions / API keys and your own BRAIN account; those costs are between you and the vendors. The wizard never starts paid inference.

  
Does automation mean submissions and income?
No. Passing screens, accepted submissions, consultant eligibility and actual payouts are four different states, and submission has its own acceptance gates.

  
Do my credentials or research get uploaded?
No. All state lives in a local SQLite ledger; exports exclude cookies, passwords and task inputs; model calls go only to providers you explicitly select.

  
How well is Windows supported?
The native tray and Task Scheduler runner work (experimental); the most mature process-isolation paths remain sandbox-exec on macOS and Docker on Linux/WSL2.
