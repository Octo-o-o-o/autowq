04Stage one · reach Gold fast

  
For anyone starting BRAIN research, or not yet at Gold. The barrier is low: install, sign in, set a budget — then hand the grind to a gated autonomous cycle that costs nothing extra, burning the tokens asleep in your subscriptions. That is exactly how the author's first week went: Gold on day 5.

  

    
STEP 1
Run the wizard once
It detects the agent CLIs you already subscribe to, writes config from your choices and launches BRAIN + provider logins — the demo above. You never repeat it.

    
STEP 2
Set budget & window
Enable research inside your own budget and authorization window. Models, research and submission are all off by default; nothing runs until you say so.

    
STEP 3
Every cycle, automated
Hypothesis → cross-provider review → bounded simulation. Research and review channels are forced apart, so the loop can't grade its own homework.

    
STEP 4
Read real outcomes
Cycle history, real receipts, failures and UNKNOWNs are all recorded as-is; failed candidates carry their reasons and never silently retry.

    
STEP 5
Gate, then submit
Qualified candidates pass an independent pre-submission acceptance gate, then you verify the platform receipt — and the next cycle tightens the loop again.

    
ALWAYS ON
Everything in the tray
Current cycle, next research / review providers, submitted alphas, real receipts and blocked states — visible and pausable at any moment.

  

  
Gold is awarded by the WorldQuant platform as alphas and OS performance accumulate; there is no fixed number of days and no "Gold mode" switch in the software. The ledger below is the author's personal history, shared for reference — not a typical outcome.

06Stage two · after Gold, keep improving

  
Gold is not the finish line, and it asks for no reinstall or new login — the same install enters its second stage: make every cycle better-informed than the last, and squeeze more verified output out of the same budget. Research quality is exactly what the platform prices its consultants on.

  

    spot a new question→name the missing evidence→collect it, bounded→run a small testable experiment→reassess when results land→feed lessons & counterexamples back↺
  

  

    
VALUE 1
Stop redoing dead research
New ideas are checked against local history and counterexamples first; failures and "unknown" stay on record — you never burn budget on the same idea in new wording.

    
VALUE 2
Make "better" testable
Improvement speaks only inside same-budget comparisons: quality retention on new data, incremental contribution inside a fixed pool. Thin evidence means keep watching — never a victory lap on one best run.

    
VALUE 3
See resources & next steps
Read it straight from Research progress in the tray: authorization deadlines, learning state, known / unknown costs, evidence gaps and the next trigger condition.

  

  
    
    
  • Suggested cadence: glance at runs, blockers and costs daily; review verified output per budget and rule counterexamples weekly; judge only after new market data arrives. (A usage suggestion — the software does not install reminder tasks for you.)
    • 
    
  • State language: "baseline" keeps the original behavior, "shadow" accumulates and compares first, "active" tries learned rankings only inside verified bounds — a learning arm in an experiment does not mean it has already improved anything.
    • 
    
  • The 14 directions are initial research topics, not fully validated or profitable strategies; you can register new topics, but they still need supporting data sources and explicit authorization, and missing material may need a source from you.
    • 
    
  • Boundaries: automated learning improves retrieval, planning and bounded experiments — it never rewrites the program, raises budgets or bypasses submission gates.
    •

08FAQ

  
Is this an official WorldQuant product?
No. autowq is an independent open-source project (Apache-2.0), unaffiliated with WorldQuant; BRAIN trademarks, platform and data belong to their owners.

  
Does autowq itself cost anything?
No — it's free and open source. It orchestrates your own model subscriptions / API keys and your own BRAIN account; those costs are between you and the vendors. The wizard never starts paid inference. Without a subscription you can start on official free API tiers (wq providers free: Gemini, OpenRouter, BigModel, SiliconFlow and more); an exhausted quota pauses and resumes automatically.

  
Does automation mean submissions and income?
No. Passing screens, accepted submissions, consultant eligibility and actual payouts are four different states, and submission has its own acceptance gates.

  
What is the tool good for after I reach Gold?
Enter stage two: the continuous-research framework turns your local history into verifiable next-step suggestions — new ideas are checked against past results and counterexamples, improvements are compared under the same budget, and authorization, costs, evidence gaps and the next trigger are all visible in Research progress. No reinstall, no re-initialization — on the first run, just pick “I am already Gold / a consultant” at the starting-point screen and go straight to the continuous-research setup.

  
Does Gold mean consultant status and income?
No. Gold is a platform tier; becoming a consultant requires an official invitation, contracting and activation; payout eligibility and actual payouts are further states, governed by the official process in your region. WorldQuant's consultant page states that Grandmaster-level consultants can potentially earn upwards of $8,000 per quarter — that is the platform's incentive structure, not a promise made by this software.

  
Do my credentials or research get uploaded?
No. All state lives in a local SQLite ledger; exports exclude cookies, passwords and task inputs; model calls go only to providers you explicitly select.

  
Full or light macOS DMG?
Both are notarized and install by dragging into Applications. The features match. The full DMG includes Python and opens ready to use. The light DMG is only the menu and the engine; first setup looks for Python 3.11+ on the machine, and the prompt tells you to install Xcode Command Line Tools if it is missing. Check for Updates opens the full DMG.

  
How well is Windows supported?
WorldQuantTray.exe alone runs the full workflow on native Windows (experimental): the wizard, tray, CLI and Task Scheduler runs live in that one file. Native Windows has no sandbox for model processes, so it supports API providers only (free presets included); to use CLI subscriptions such as Claude Code or Codex, deploy the Linux + Docker setup inside WSL2.
